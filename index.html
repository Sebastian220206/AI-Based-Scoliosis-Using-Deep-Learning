Clinical Severity Classification

NORMAL

Cobb Angle < 10° — Perfect Alignment.

Complexity made simple — so you stay in control. At this stage, the spine shows no significant curvature, representing optimal spinal health and skeletal symmetry.

We believe powerful diagnostic tools should be easy to use, providing peace of mind through precise measurement.

MILD

Cobb Angle 10° – 25° — Early Detection.

Built for growth — designed for ambition. Mild scoliosis requires consistent clinical observation to monitor potential progression during growth phases.

Early screening ensures that even subtle shifts are captured, allowing for proactive health management.

MODERATE

Cobb Angle 25° – 40° — Active Intervention.

Serious performance behind every click. Moderate cases often necessitate bracing or specialized physical therapy to prevent further curvature increase.

Our platform provides high-precision tracking, delivering enterprise-grade stability for complex clinical decisions.

SEVERE

Cobb Angle > 40° — Clinical Priority.

Ultimate precision for critical outcomes. Severe scoliosis often requires surgical evaluation to correct alignment and protect respiratory and cardiac function.

From automated detection to surgical planning, ScolioAI gives you unmatched capability and control at your fingertips.

Risk Mitigation & Clinical Guidance

RISKS

Physiological & Structural Compromise.

Untreated spinal curvature poses significant long-term health challenges. Beyond physical aesthetics, the structural impact can lead to systemic complications.

  • • Pulmonary Stress: Reduced thoracic space impacting lung function.
  • • Chronic Pain: Nerve compression and persistent muscular fatigue.
  • • Mobility: Progressive loss of flexibility and structural balance.

SAFETY

Evidence-Based Clinical Precautions.

Early detection is the primary defense against progression. Implementing a structured monitoring plan ensures timely intervention and optimal outcomes.

  • • Digital Screening: AI-powered periodic posture assessment.
  • • Physical Therapy: Target muscular strengthening and stabilization.
  • • Clinical Oversight: Coordination with orthopedic specialists.
